01 — About NSH

A holding company that builds what it originates.

Ntsika Shembe Holdings (Pty) Ltd is an IP origination holding company based in Vereeniging, Gauteng, South Africa. We originate proprietary technology and intellectual property — taking ideas from first concept through to live, working products.

Our flagship platform, Culture Share, demonstrates the approach in practice: applied AI, structured data, and full-stack engineering combined into a single working platform, built and deployed end to end by the NSH team.

The company is led by its Founder & Executive Director, supported by a growing executive team across finance, legal, and technology.

02 — Flagship platform

Culture Share

A live cultural technology platform combining social connection, a peer-reviewed cultural knowledge base, and applied AI — built and deployed end to end by the NSH team.

AI-powered moderation

Automated content moderation with decade- and topic-based classification, tuned for culturally sensitive material and running on real user content today.

Personalised discovery

A “For You” feed built on a personalisation engine that learns from user behaviour across the platform’s cultural knowledge base.

In-platform AI agent

A conversational AI agent embedded directly in the product, working against a structured, peer-reviewed content base rather than generic chat.

Closed-loop points economy

CST, a closed-loop loyalty ledger built with financial-governance discipline — not a cryptocurrency, but a real digital incentive system running in production.

Conversational AI & Agent Development

AI agents embedded in live platforms, built against structured, domain-specific knowledge bases rather than generic chat wrappers.

Custom Software & Platform Engineering

Full-stack design and build of production platforms — backend architecture, authentication, community layers, and mobile packaging — from concept through live deployment.

Data Infrastructure & Digital Economy Systems
